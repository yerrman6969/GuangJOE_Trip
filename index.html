Essential Tips
💳
Payment
Alipay or WeChat Pay everywhere. Cash rarely accepted. Set up before leaving Bangkok.
🗺️
Navigation
Google Maps doesn't work in China. Use Amap (Gaode) or Apple Maps. Download offline before flying.
🚇
MRT Key Lines
Line 1: Tianhe ↔ Shamian (west). Line 8: Changgang (GAFA). Signs and announcements in English.
🌡️
Heat Strategy
Sketch outdoors before 11 AM or after 5 PM only. 12–3 PM: AC indoors (mall, museum). Carry cooling towel + SPF50.
🫰
Yum Cha Etiquette
2-finger tap = thank you when tea poured (peer level). Tilt teapot lid open = need refill. Say "唔该" (m̀h'gōi) to thank staff.
🚲
Bike Sharing
HelloBike (blue) via Alipay — supports international numbers. ~1.5 RMB per 30 min. Only ride early AM or after 5 PM in August.
📱
Xiaohongshu
Use your account to find local spots, restaurants, and art supply shops. Search "广州" + what you need. Better than any travel guide.
🛡️
Language
Cantonese is primary in local shops — not Mandarin. Use Google Translate camera mode to read menus. Staff at malls and MRT speak some English.
Flight
BKK → Guangzhou (CAN)
~3 hrs direct. Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport.
